OUR STANDARD

Evidence over hype.

Three separate questions. No blanket “verified” claims.

01 / Attribution

Was REA actually used?

We link to the primary-source README, investigation notes or author statement. General reverse-engineering projects are not quietly presented as REA builds.

02 / Artifact

What was actually published?

We distinguish full applications, research experiments, official showcases, releases, tutorials and integration tools. A source-linked study is not a finished rebuild.

03 / Execution

Who tested the result?

Author-reported tests and independent reproduction are different. Every passport states what remains unknown, including compatibility and legal limitations.

Important: this is an independent community catalog, not affiliated with or endorsed by the developers of REA or any project studied. Research and software reuse must respect applicable laws, licenses, privacy and rights.
