# Growth and launch plan

## Positioning
Awesome REA Builds is an evidence-linked, implementation-first atlas—not a generic reverse-engineering link dump.

## First 30 days
- Week 1: launch the source-backed 44-case catalog; clean README, working static site and contribution policy.
- Week 2: author three exceptional Build Passports with precise tested environments, limitations, citations, and reproducible commands where possible.
- Week 3: publish technical write-ups describing specific REA findings and launch a community correction campaign.
- Week 4: publish a weekly spotlight, review new submissions, measure quality contributions and return visitors.

## Distribution
- Link concrete technical case studies in REA community discussions where welcome.
- Submit relevant technical discoveries to Hacker News and engineering subreddits following posting rules.
- Invite featured maintainers to verify/correct their project's description (no unsolicited star requests).
- Apply to high-quality awesome directories once differentiation, curation and maintenance are established.
- Publish updates with RSS if readership merits it.

## Metrics
Stars: 100 → 300 → 500 → 1,000 are milestone goals, **not guaranteed forecasts**. Also measure qualified submissions, source corrections, recurring visitors, reproduction evidence, and click-through to original projects.

## Integrity
No paid placements, fabricated benchmark claims, bot stars or fake "verified" status. Catalog entries are never auto-accepted from crawler output.
